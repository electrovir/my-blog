As I predicted in my [first blog post](https://electrovir.com/2024-05-27-how-i-created-this-blog), I have tired of Docusaurus and wrote my own blog generator: [blog-vir](https://www.npmjs.com/package/blog-vir).

<!-- truncate -->

Here's how I converted to it:

1. Install `blog-vir` and use its build and dev CLI commands.
2. Move posts to [`posts/`](https://github.com/electrovir/my-blog/tree/328cf7b088536081779a60303725d4c7620d6ba4/posts) and public files to [`www-static/`](https://github.com/electrovir/my-blog/tree/328cf7b088536081779a60303725d4c7620d6ba4/www-static).
3. Create [`configs/blog-vir.config.ts`](https://github.com/electrovir/my-blog/blob/328cf7b088536081779a60303725d4c7620d6ba4/configs/blog-vir.config.ts) and [`src/my-blog.element.ts`](https://github.com/electrovir/my-blog/blob/328cf7b088536081779a60303725d4c7620d6ba4/src/my-blog.element.ts).
4. Remove many now-unnecessary configs, dependencies, and scripts.

This blog is now much lighter, easier to configure, and isn't stuck in Docusaurus dependency hell with all its accompanying npm audit reports.
