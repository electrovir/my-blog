---
title: How to Reset a 7th Generation iPod Touch
tags: [hardware]
---

Nearly all guides online explain how to reset an iPod Touch incorrectly for the 7th generation. Here's the correct way. While Apple documents this themselves, it was surprisingly difficult to track down, so I'm reiterating it here in case someone (like myself) needs it again.

You do not need to have your device shut off (as most guides instruct) before starting these steps. In fact, keeping it on makes it easier to see that this is working.

1. Plug the iPod into your computer via USB.
2. Hold down the power button and _volume down_ button.
    - All guides say to use power and home buttons. That does not work on 7th gen iPod Touch.
3. Once the Apple logo shows up (like it's booting up), let go of the power button. Continue holding the volume down button.
4. Once you see a connect to computer graphic (Lightning cord + laptop), then you can release the volume down button.
